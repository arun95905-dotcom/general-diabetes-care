WHEN TO SEEK CARE

When Should You Consult?

Diabetes may be detected during a routine blood test or may cause symptoms when blood sugar becomes high or low. Early evaluation and regular follow-up can help maintain better control.

Newly Detected High Blood Sugar

If your blood sugar or HbA1c is higher than normal, proper evaluation can confirm diabetes or prediabetes and guide treatment.

Symptoms of High Blood Sugar

Frequent urination, increased thirst, tiredness, unexplained weight loss or blurred vision may be signs of high blood sugar.

Diabetes Not Under Control

If sugar levels remain high or fluctuate despite treatment, your medicines, diet and other possible causes can be reviewed.

Repeated Low Blood Sugar

Sweating, trembling, dizziness or sudden weakness may occur with low sugar and may require adjustment of medicines or insulin.

Starting or Adjusting Insulin

Insulin may be required in some people with diabetes. Dose, timing, glucose monitoring and prevention of low sugar can be reviewed.

Regular Diabetes Review

Regular follow-up helps check blood sugar control, medicines and early signs of diabetes-related health problems.

DIABETES CARE

Individualised Diabetes Management

Diabetes treatment is different for every person. Care is planned according to the type of diabetes, sugar levels, lifestyle, medicines and other health conditions.

Type 1 Diabetes

Regular insulin treatment, glucose monitoring and dose review help maintain good control and reduce high or low sugar episodes.

Type 2 Diabetes

Treatment is planned according to your sugar levels and overall health using lifestyle changes, medicines or insulin when needed.

Prediabetes

Early lifestyle changes, weight control and regular monitoring can reduce the chance of progressing from prediabetes to diabetes.

Gestational Diabetes

Blood sugar during pregnancy is carefully monitored and treated to support the health of both the mother and developing baby.

Uncontrolled Diabetes

Persistently high blood sugar or HbA1c is evaluated and the treatment plan is adjusted to improve diabetes control.

Medication & Insulin Review

Tablets, injections or insulin doses are reviewed according to sugar levels, kidney function and risk of low blood sugar.

HbA1c & Glucose Monitoring

Fasting, post-meal and HbA1c results are reviewed together to understand how well diabetes is controlled over time.

LONG-TERM DIABETES CARE

Diabetes Complication Screening

Diabetes can gradually affect different organs even before symptoms become obvious. Regular screening helps identify early changes and allows appropriate treatment when needed.

Kidney Health

Blood and urine tests help detect early kidney changes from diabetes, so treatment can begin before problems become advanced.

Eye Health

Regular diabetic eye examination helps detect retinal changes early, sometimes before a person notices problems with vision.

Nerve & Foot Health

Feet and sensation are checked for numbness, burning, wounds or reduced sensation that may occur due to diabetic nerve problems.

Heart & Blood Vessel Health

Blood pressure, cholesterol and other risk factors are assessed because diabetes increases the long-term risk of heart attack and stroke.

Weight & Metabolic Health

Body weight and cholesterol are monitored because they can influence blood sugar control as well as future heart health.

Overall Diabetes Review

Blood sugar, HbA1c, medicines and complication screening results are reviewed together to plan ongoing treatment.

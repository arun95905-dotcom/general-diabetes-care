DIABETES CARE

Individualised Diabetes Management

Diabetes treatment is more than controlling a single blood sugar reading. Regular assessment, appropriate medication, lifestyle measures and screening for diabetes-related complications are important parts of long-term care.

🩸

Type 2 Diabetes

Evaluation and ongoing management of type 2 diabetes, including treatment review and blood glucose monitoring.

📊

Prediabetes

Assessment of increased diabetes risk with guidance on lifestyle measures, weight management and follow-up.

🤰

Gestational Diabetes

Evaluation and management of diabetes detected during pregnancy with appropriate glucose monitoring and follow-up.

📈

Uncontrolled Diabetes

Review of persistently elevated blood glucose or HbA1c and adjustment of the diabetes treatment plan when required.

💊

Medication Review

Review of oral diabetes medicines, injectable treatments and insulin therapy according to individual clinical needs.

🧪

HbA1c Monitoring

Regular assessment of long-term glucose control and treatment response using HbA1c and blood glucose results.

REGULAR MONITORING

Comprehensive Diabetes Follow-up

Follow-up is planned according to the person's blood sugar control, current medicines, associated medical conditions and risk of diabetes-related complications.

✓ Fasting and post-meal blood glucose review
✓ HbA1c monitoring
✓ Diabetes medication review
✓ Insulin treatment review when required
✓ Blood pressure monitoring
✓ Cholesterol and cardiovascular risk assessment
✓ Kidney function and urine protein screening
✓ Neuropathy and foot-risk assessment
✓ Lifestyle and dietary counselling
✓ Weight and metabolic risk assessment

LONG-TERM HEALTH

Diabetes Complication Screening

Long-standing or poorly controlled diabetes can affect several organs. Appropriate screening helps identify problems early and allows timely management or specialist referral when required.

🫘

Kidney Health

Monitoring kidney function and urine albumin to assess the risk of diabetic kidney disease.

👁️

Eye Health

Guidance regarding periodic diabetic eye examination for early detection of diabetic retinopathy.

🦶

Foot & Nerve Health

Assessment for numbness, altered sensation and other features associated with diabetic neuropathy.

❤️

Cardiovascular Risk

Assessment of blood pressure, cholesterol and other cardiovascular risk factors associated with diabetes.

WHEN TO SEEK CARE

When Should You Consult?

🧪

Newly Detected High Sugar

If a blood glucose or HbA1c test is elevated, further evaluation can confirm the diagnosis and guide treatment.

💧

Symptoms of High Blood Sugar

Increased thirst, frequent urination, unexplained weight loss, fatigue or blurred vision may require evaluation.

📉

Recurrent Low Blood Sugar

Repeated episodes of low glucose may require adjustment of medicines, insulin, meals or monitoring.

📋

Regular Diabetes Review

Periodic follow-up helps assess glucose control, medications and screening requirements.

Clinical Experience

Dr. ARUNKUMAR has nearly 8 years of experience in general medical practice, providing evaluation and management of a broad range of acute and chronic medical conditions.

With around 4 years of focused experience in diabetes care, particular clinical interest includes:

  • Diabetes mellitus and prediabetes
  • Gestational diabetes
  • Diabetes prevention and lifestyle management
  • Individualized diabetes treatment
  • Monitoring and prevention of diabetes-related complications
  • Hypertension and cardiovascular risk assessment
  • Thyroid and other metabolic disorders
